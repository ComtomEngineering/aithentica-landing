## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor, transparency, and authoritative clarity demanded by enterprise IP law, provenance auditing, and AI compliance. The design philosophy unites modern enterprise SaaS with high-assurance legal-tech engineering: every surface communicates forensic accuracy, audit-readiness, and non-negotiable trust.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for General Counsels, Chief Risk Officers, IP Attorneys, and Chief AI Officers. The UI elicits confidence, analytical calm, institutional credibility, and decisive risk awareness. It eschews generic startup softness in favor of architectural precision, uncompromising legibility, and exact data hierarchies.

### Aesthetic Methodology: Crisp Corporate Precision
- **Pristine White & Structural Neutrals**: High-clarity backgrounds built on layered cold zinc and slate surfaces, avoiding warm or muddy undertones.
- **Structural Outlines & Ghost Borders**: Strict 1px hairline borders that ground visual components, echo technical ledgers, and maintain structural geometry.
- **Controlled Semantic Accents**: Functional cobalt blue indicates executive actions and audit trails; authoritative emerald green confirms verified human provenance; calibrated amber and rose instantly isolate regulatory and copyright risk vectors.

## Colors

The palette establishes an unshakeable hierarchy anchored in cold slate and deep oceanic cobalt. All functional roles prioritize high-contrast luminance differentials meeting WCAG AAA requirements for data-critical tables, compliance badges, and statutory risk states.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0369A1`)**: Cobalt Precision. Reserved for primary executive actions, current audit pathway nodes, table filter active states, and focal verification controls.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Ink Slate. Acts as the structural anchor for root container headers, primary narrative typography, and critical operational toggles.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`)**: Provenance Emerald. Dedicated to positive verification: "Verified Human Authorship", zero-infringement certifications, and legally attested logs.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Structural Slate. Scales from base canvas backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`) to precise hairlines (`#E2E8F0`, `#CBD5E1`) and secondary meta-labels (`#475569`).

### Diagnostic & Risk Semantics
- **Warning / Ambiguity Alert (`#D97706`)**: AI-assisted synthetic content requiring human-in-the-loop review, ambiguous licensing clauses, or contested training weights.
- **Critical Violation / Risk (`#E11D48`)**: Direct copyright infringement match, unauthorized model fine-tuning trace, or failed compliance checkpoints.
- **Surface Elevation Layers**: Canvas ground is `#F8FAFC`; elevated table and card surfaces use pure `#FFFFFF` with hairlines in `#E2E8F0`; inset wells and audit track containers utilize `#F1F5F9`.

## Typography

Typography balances commanding architectural display forms with hyper-legible body text and analytical code-grade metadata.

### Typographic Hierarchies & Pairings
- **Headlines (Hanken Grotesk)**: Chosen for its razor-sharp geometric precision, square-like structural rhythm, and modern editorial authority. Headlines convey executive institutional assurance without superfluous embellishment.
- **Body Text (Inter)**: The standard for high-density enterprise platforms. Used for legal contract disclaimers, compliance criteria, narrative evidence, and report text. It offers zero visual friction across complex nested grids.
- **Metadata & Data Points (JetBrains Mono)**: Integral to the legal-tech experience. Applied across hash fingerprints, cryptographically signed ledger IDs, risk scoring percentages, timestamped audit chains, and file checksums.

### Rendering Rules
- Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be enabled on all numerical representations, scores, and monetary liabilities.
- Label styles utilize deliberate uppercase tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) for compliance status tags, cryptographic verification states, and risk indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure mirrors high-throughput forensic workstations: organized, repeatable, and mathematically predictable. It employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to an 8px base rhythm.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid with a fixed max-width container of 1600px. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (24px) with canvas margins at `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Gutters compress to `1rem` (16px); section margins stay at `1.5rem` (24px). Split compliance panels collapse from 50/50 dual view to stacked inspector modes.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout. Margins collapse to `1rem` (16px) with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters. Deep telemetry graphs and split-screen comparison tools degrade gracefully into tabbed sequential inspectors.

### Density & Rhythmic Cadence
- **Dense Data Zones**: Table rows, property sheets, and audit timelines use strict 4px/8px stepping (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to support maximal visible evidence per viewport.
- **Structural Zones**: Workspace headers, risk executive summaries, and multi-file ingestion zones utilize `space-lg` and `space-xl` to prevent cognitive fatigue during prolonged legal audits.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on intense dropshadows or theatrical blur filters. Depth is conveyed through clean structural layering, deliberate tonal shifts, and low-opacity slate outlines that emphasize stability and order.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Canvas Base (Level 0)**: `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for all screens.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF`. All active workspace modules, data tables, audit dossiers, and compliance forms rest here. Defined by a crisp 1px perimeter border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Popovers (Level 2)**: `#FFFFFF`. Dropdown menus, risk tooltip definitions, and contextual actions. Outlined with `#CBD5E1` and accompanied by a focused shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Dialogs & Forensic Drill-downs (Level 3)**: `#FFFFFF`. Centered analysis viewports. Paired with a backdrop dim of `#0F172A` at 40% opacity and an authoritative drop shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
5. **Inset Depths (Recessed Level)**: `#F1F5F9`. Code blocks, raw model prompt logs, and JSON license certificates feature an inset background with an interior stroke: `inset 0 0 0 1px #E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The design system uses deliberate, disciplined corner radii (Level 1: Soft). This reinforces a no-nonsense engineering aesthetic, maintaining clear separation between containers without appearing aggressive or toy-like.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radii (`0.125rem` / 2px)**: Badges, progress bar tracks, and segmented control nodes.
- **Base Components (`0.25rem` / 4px)**: Buttons, inputs, dropdown triggers, and risk chips.
- **Structural Modules (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Cards, audit panels, ingestion dropzones, and tabular data containers.
- **Full Radius (`9999px`)**: Provenance verification check indicators and status indicator dots only.

## Components

Components prioritize precision, high information density, and unambiguous interaction states.

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Cobalt (`#0369A1`) with text in `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#0284C7`. Active: `#075985`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#38BDF8`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Outline**: Solid `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border of `#CBD5E1` and text in `#0F172A`. Hover: `#F8FAFC` background with border `#94A3B8`.
- **Destructive**: Hairline `#FECDD3` border, background `#FFF1F2`, text `#BE123C`. Used strictly for revoking licenses or flagging IP breaches.
- **Typography & Dimensions**: Height 36px (compact) or 40px (standard). Text set in `Inter` semi-bold 13px/14px.

### Badges & Provenance Chips
- **Human Authorship Verified**: Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, paired with an emerald `#059669` leading check icon.
- **AI Synthetic Trace Detected**: Background `#FEF3C7`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
- **Copyright Infringement Warning**: Background `#FFE4E6`, border `#FECDD3`, text `#9F1239`.
- **Styling**: `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase (`label-md`), 2px border radius, exact horizontal padding of 8px, vertical padding of 2px.

### Data Tables & Audit Logs
- **Header**: Height 36px, background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Typography in `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase `#475569`.
- **Rows**: Alternating subtle hover states (`#F8FAFC` on hover). Row height standard 48px, compact 36px. Border-bottom 1px solid `#F1F5F9`.
- **Interactive Cells**: Forensic diffs show red/green line indicators on the left border (2px) of affected cells.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Ground**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Active Focus**: Border changes to `#0369A1` accompanied by a crisp `0 0 0 1px #0369A1` glow ring. No heavy blurred focus drops.
- **Error State**: Border `#E11D48` with helper text rendered in `Inter` 12px `#E11D48`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry**: 16px square checkboxes with 2px corner radius; 16px circular radio buttons.
- **Borders**: 1.5px border `#94A3B8`. Checked state transitions immediately to `#0369A1` background with a `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### Specialized Platform Components
- **Copyright Risk Meter**: A segmented micro-bar (4 segments) visualizing threat levels from green (cleared) through amber to critical crimson.
- **Cryptographic Hash Pill**: Inset `#F1F5F9` background, `#E2E8F0` border, `JetBrains Mono` text with click-to-copy SHA-256 validation triggers.
- **Side-by-Side Ingestion Inspector**: A dual-pane split viewport comparing original copyright-registered assets with AI-generated outputs, highlighting syntactic and semantic overlap using persistent color highlights.
